Marketing Campaign Insights & Automation

Leveraging data, automation, and predictive modeling to enhance marketing effectiveness.

Project Overview

This project was initiated to solve a critical business challenge: the marketing team needed to move from manual, time-consuming campaign analysis to an automated, data-driven strategy. My objective was to harness data analytics to refine marketing tactics, automate performance tracking, and build predictive models to drive measurable improvements in engagement and ROI, all while adhering to strict HIPAA and GDPR data privacy standards.

Example Marketing Analytics Dashboard Visualization

My Contributions & Key Achievements

Automation for Peak Efficiency

I developed automated performance tracking workflows using Apache Airflow and AWS Glue. This initiative eliminated hours of manual reporting, reduced the risk of human error, and significantly improved the consistency and reliability of our data.

Predictive Insights for Proactive Strategy

Using Python and libraries like Scikit-learn, I designed and implemented predictive models to forecast campaign outcomes. This empowered the marketing team to make proactive strategy adjustments, directly contributing to a 10% enhancement in campaign Return on Investment (ROI).

Intuitive Dashboards for Clear Insights

I created a suite of intuitive Tableau dashboards that provided stakeholders with real-time access to Key Performance Indicators (KPIs). By translating complex data into clear visualizations, we streamlined the overall decision-making process for marketing strategies.

Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: One key challenge was integrating data from disparate sources (e.g., Google Analytics, internal CRM) while maintaining accuracy and compliance.

Solution: This was addressed by implementing robust ETL processes with AWS Glue and setting up rigorous data validation checks within the Airflow DAGs. This ensured a single source of truth for all reporting and analysis.
